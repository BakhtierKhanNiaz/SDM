SDM

Sends this page to SDM, which asks the quality (720p, 1080p, 4K or audio).

A small SDM button appears at the top-right of a video while it plays or under the mouse. The browser asks first, because the button needs to see where videos are on web pages. It sends SDM only the page's address, and only when you click it.

On: downloads your browser starts go to SDM instead, and SDM asks you to confirm each one. Off: downloads stay in the browser; right-click a link and choose Download with SDM. Private windows are never affected. If SDM can't take a download, the browser downloads it as usual.
